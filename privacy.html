Privacy policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

SatoshiPulse is a personal tool that runs only on its owner's own computer. This policy covers the access it asks for when its owner connects a YouTube account.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google's youtube.force-ssl scope), SatoshiPulse uploads the daily video it makes to the owner's own YouTube channel, adds caption tracks to that video, creates one playlist and adds the video to it. It reads the channel's name to confirm which channel is connected. It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments or account data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token and the videos are stored only on the owner's computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party; the only data sent to Google is the video, its title, description and captions, uploaded to the owner's channel.

Sharing and selling

No data is shared, sold or used for advertising. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can disconnect YouTube in SatoshiPulse's settings, or revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
