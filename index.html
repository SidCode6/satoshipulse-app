SatoshiPulse

A personal tool that runs on its owner's own Mac.

Each morning SatoshiPulse finds what podcasts on YouTube said about Bitcoin the day before, with a timestamped link to each moment. It can also stitch those moments into one short video and upload it, unlisted, to its owner's own YouTube channel, with captions, into a playlist.

It is not a public service: there is no sign-up, and it is used only by the person who runs it.
